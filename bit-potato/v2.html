the living potato

BitPotato v2. The potato lives now. It hungers. It bakes. And sometimes, it leaves a baby.

it hungers

The potato starts with 48 hours of life. Every $1 steal feeds it +4 hours. If Hunger hits zero, the potato starves, and the whole pot rolls into the next round. The dead potato feeds the next crop.

it bakes

When the pot crosses the Bake Threshold ($50, climbing $25 every round), the potato evolves. 70% pays out to the holder and everyone who ever held it. 20% seeds the next round. 10% goes to the baby pot.

it reproduces

Every bake, there's a 25% chance the potato leaves a baby. The baby pot keeps growing until it's born, then one random past holder takes the whole thing. The longer it goes unborn, the bigger it gets.

it sustains itself

$20 seed, once. After that the game feeds itself forever. Bake reserves and starvation rollovers mean the potato never needs another dollar from anyone but the players.
