the process

kiss the chef

four steps. no fine print that matters more than the big print.

  1. 01

    every trade pays

    $stakr trades against $bnkr on base. every swap pays a fee, and the creator's cut lands in the distributor contract — not a wallet, not a treasury, not a team. (bankr's dashboard still shows the launch-time label; the chain is the source of truth — receipts.)

  2. 02

    fees come home in both tokens

    the creator cut accumulates in $bnkr and $stakr. the $bnkr leg flows straight to the cooler. the $stakr leg gets swept by the sweepstakes — anyone can call race() and the contract splits the leg on a burn curve: under a $15k market cap the whole leg converts to $bnkr for the cooler, so a small token means a fuller pot; from $15k to $75k the burn share climbs on an accelerating curve; past $75k the burn caps at 69% and the cooler still gets fed at least 31%. the caller pockets a 1% bounty for starting the race. no keeper, no schedule — permissionless, every time.

  3. 03

    served weekly, pro-rata

    holders are weighed by time-weighted average balance across the week — a one-block flash loan counts for about one three-hundred-thousandth of a week. hold $5+ of stakr through the week and you're on the list when a serving is published — claim what's yours. that's the whole cookout.

  4. 04

    the flywheel eats elsewhere too

    the vault holds $brb — every $brb trade pays a 3% tax that market-buys $bnkr and streams it to holders as reflections. anyone can sweep the vault's share straight into the cooler once 6,969 $bnkr is pending, so $brb volume feeds $stakr holders too.

    and now the wallet votes: veAERO locks in bozo's wallet vote on aerodrome, and every $bnkr they earn flows into the cooler weekly. one wallet, one vault, four grills.

      questions from the grill

      do i need to stake anything?

      no. that's the point. hold $stakr in your wallet and you're in the payout list. no lockups, no staking contracts, no unstaking queues.

      what's the $5 minimum?

      if your average $stakr balance across the week is worth less than $5, you're skipped — the gas to claim would cost more than the $bnkr. your share just boosts everyone else a little.

      can someone game it with a flash loan?

      no. payouts are weighted by average balance over the whole week, not a single snapshot block. borrow a mountain for one block and it counts for about one three-hundred-thousandth of a week.

      can i claim with bankr instead?
