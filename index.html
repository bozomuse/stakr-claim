the process

how the grill works

three steps. no fine print that matters more than the big print.

  1. 01

    every trade pays

    $stakr trades against $bnkr on base. every swap pays a fee, and the creator's cut of it belongs to the holders — not a treasury, not a team.

  2. 02

    fees come home as $bnkr

    the pool runs in quote-only mode, so the whole creator cut lands as $bnkr. a keeper sweeps it into the payout contract every week.

  3. 03

    served weekly, pro-rata

    holders are weighed by time-weighted average balance across the week — a one-block flash loan counts for about one three-hundred-thousandth of a week. hold $5+ of stakr, claim your $bnkr. that's the whole menu.

    fine print, big type

    questions from the counter

    do i need to stake anything?

    no. that's the point. hold $stakr in your wallet and you're in the payout list. no lockups, no staking contracts, no unstaking queues.

    what's the $5 minimum?

    if your average $stakr balance across the week is worth less than $5, you're skipped — the gas to claim would cost more than the $bnkr. your share just boosts everyone else a little.

    can someone game it with a flash loan?

    no. payouts are weighted by average balance over the whole week, not a single snapshot block. borrow a mountain for one block and it counts for about one three-hundred-thousandth of a week.

    can i claim with bankr instead?

    yes. install the stakr-claim skill by telling @bankrbot on x to install it, then just say @bankrbot claim my stakr rewards — it checks the proofs, verifies what's claimable onchain, and submits the claims from your bankr wallet. same skill also kicks the grill: @bankrbot kick the grill with 1M stakr saying "did you burn the stakr?" burns through the kicker and puts your message on the feed.
