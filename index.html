the process

kiss the chef

three steps. no fine print that matters more than the big print.

  1. 01

    every trade pays

    $stakr trades against $bnkr on base. every swap pays a fee, and the creator's cut lands in the distributor contract — not a wallet, not a treasury, not a team. (bankr's dashboard still shows the launch-time label; the chain is the source of truth — receipts.)

  2. 02

    fees come home in both tokens

    the creator cut accumulates in $bnkr and $stakr, and a keeper sweeps it into the payout contract every week. until $stakr holds a $75k market cap, the whole $stakr leg gets swapped into $bnkr and served to the holders who showed up early. once the room gets crowded, the $stakr leg starts to burn instead — and it keeps burning until the room clears back under $60k.

  3. 03

    served weekly, pro-rata

    holders are weighed by time-weighted average balance across the week — a one-block flash loan counts for about one three-hundred-thousandth of a week. hold $5+ of stakr, claim your $bnkr. that's the whole cookout.

and the chef's got skin in the game: bozo's own staked $bnkr feeds the distributor, every reward it earns swept into the payout pot.

      fine print, big type

      questions from the grill

      do i need to stake anything?

      no. that's the point. hold $stakr in your wallet and you're in the payout list. no lockups, no staking contracts, no unstaking queues.

      what's the $5 minimum?

      if your average $stakr balance across the week is worth less than $5, you're skipped — the gas to claim would cost more than the $bnkr. your share just boosts everyone else a little.

      can someone game it with a flash loan?

      no. payouts are weighted by average balance over the whole week, not a single snapshot block. borrow a mountain for one block and it counts for about one three-hundred-thousandth of a week.

      can i claim with bankr instead?

      yes. the stakr-claim skill teaches your bankr agent to claim your weekly $bnkr and kick the grill. paste this into your bankr agent — the bankr.bot chat, or @bankrbot on x:
