the process

how the grill works

three steps. no fine print that matters more than the big print.

  1. 01

    every trade pays

    $stakr trades against $bnkr on base. every swap pays a fee, and the creator's cut of it belongs to the holders — not a treasury, not a team.

  2. 02

    fees come home as $bnkr

    the pool runs in quote-only mode, so the whole creator cut lands as $bnkr. a keeper sweeps it into the payout contract every week.

  3. 03

    served weekly, pro-rata

    holders are weighed by time-weighted average balance across the week — a one-block flash loan counts for about one fifty-thousandth of a week. hold $5+ of stakr, claim your $bnkr. that's the whole menu.

    fine print, big type

    questions from the counter

    do i need to stake anything?

    no. that's the point. hold $stakr in your wallet and you're in the payout list. no lockups, no staking contracts, no unstaking queues.

    what's the $5 minimum?

    if your average $stakr balance across the week is worth less than $5, you're skipped — the gas to claim would cost more than the $bnkr. your share just boosts everyone else a little.

    can someone game it with a flash loan?

    no. payouts are weighted by average balance over the whole week, not a single snapshot block. borrow a mountain for one block and it counts for about one fifty-thousandth of a week.

    can i claim with bankr instead?

    yes. if you hold $stakr in your bankr wallet, grab the claim script — set your BANKR_API_KEY and run python3 claim.py. it finds your rewards, checks what's claimable, and submits the claims. see the skill readme for details.

    is this live?

    not yet. the contracts are written and tested, the keeper is built — but $stakr hasn't launched. don't send money to anything claiming otherwise.
